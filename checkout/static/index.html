What this page is

A merchant, running as its own service on its own port, integrating against the routing API. Your browser never talks to the router — this page posts to the merchant, and the merchant calls the router server-to-server, which is how a real integration works.
Routing decisions are made per issuing bank, so net banking is the method wired up here. No card details are collected anywhere.
